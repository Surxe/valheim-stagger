How this is calculated
  • Stagger limit = max health × the creature's stagger factor. Max health = base × (stars + 1), so a 2★ enemy needs 3× the stagger of a 0★ one.
  • Each hit adds (blunt + slash + pierce + lightning) after the attack's damage multiplier (combo finisher 2×, secondaries), the random skill roll, the creature's resistances, world-level armor and multiplayer scaling, then × the attack's stagger multiplier.
  • Decay: the meter drains by limit / 5 every second (full meter empties in 5 s). Attacks repeat their combo with the wiki-measured swing timings, nonstop, starting from an empty meter. "Never" means the meter plateaus below the limit or is still short after 60 s.
  • Skill roll: damage × a random factor in [lerp(0.4, 1, skill/100) ± 0.15], clamped to 0–1. "Low" is the guaranteed case (0.85 at skill 100).
  • Not modeled: sneak-attack bonus, weak points, multi-target penalties, status effects (e.g. Fader's power), parries (instant stagger), the double damage while already staggered, hits that miss.
  • Formulas come from the decompiled game code (Character.AddStaggerDamage, UpdateStagger, ApplyDamage). The per-creature and per-weapon numbers are from the wiki and still need verification against game data.
